Welcome to Beautiful Iowa

This is the home of Beautiful Iowa, a YouTube channel showcasing the Hawkeye State the way Iowans see it — river bluffs at sunrise, state parks in every season, small-town main streets, back roads, and the hidden corners most people drive past for years before finally stopping.

We film the Iowa that is still standing: the overlooks and the waterfalls, the courthouses and the covered bridges, the quiet places and the famous ones. Come see why we love it here.

Watch on YouTube — @BeautifulIowa

Beautiful Iowa is run from Jasper County, Iowa. It is the video companion of Iowa, On This Day, a history page that takes one Iowa town at a time — its old newspapers, its photographs, and the names in its records.
