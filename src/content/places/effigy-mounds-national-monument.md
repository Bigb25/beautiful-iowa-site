High on the bluffs above the Mississippi River in Allamakee County, Effigy
Mounds National Monument preserves a landscape found almost nowhere else: a
series of Native American mounds, many of them built in the shapes of bears
and birds. The effigy mounds lie along the wooded ridgetops, quiet under the
trees, above one of the widest and most beautiful stretches of the river.

The overlook at Fire Point is the view we judge every other overlook against.
From the top, the Mississippi opens out below in channels and islands, and at
sunrise — the photograph above — the whole valley fills with light. It is the
scene we chose for the front of this site and for the channel banner, because
it holds the whole Beautiful Iowa idea in a single frame: river, bluffs, and
high ground, worth the climb every time.
