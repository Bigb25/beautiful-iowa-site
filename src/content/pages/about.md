Beautiful Iowa is a showcase of the state we live in: river bluffs at sunrise,
state parks in every season, small-town main streets, back roads, and the
hidden corners most people drive past for years before finally stopping. We
film and photograph the Iowa that is still standing — the overlooks and the
waterfalls, the courthouses and the covered bridges, the quiet places and the
famous ones.

Three things live here, under one roof:

- **The films**, on the Beautiful Iowa channel on YouTube: town stories told
  from the photographs and records a place left behind, and field films from
  the road.
- **The town archive**, the permanent home of Iowa, On This Day: every town
  week's stories, photographs, and records, republished here where they can be
  searched and kept.
- **The field notes**, a blog of the places we go: the stops a drive turns
  into, written up with our own photographs.

Everything historical here is drawn from public sources — the Library of
Congress, public archives, and libraries — and every image carries its source,
so anything you find can be checked against the original.

Beautiful Iowa is run from Jasper County, Iowa. Write to us at
b.dealwatch@gmail.com.
