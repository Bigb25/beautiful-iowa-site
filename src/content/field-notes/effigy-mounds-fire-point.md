**This is a sample entry**, placed so the field notes have their shape before
the first real stop is written up.

Some places explain a whole project. Fire Point, in Effigy Mounds National
Monument above the Mississippi, is the view we chose to stand for Beautiful
Iowa: the river running between wooded bluffs, the backwaters spread out
below, and the quiet sense that this corner of the state has been watched over
for a very long time. The monument preserves more than two hundred Native
American mounds, including the great bear-shaped effigies this stretch of the
river is known for.

Field notes are the written half of the road work: when a drive turns into a
stop — a park, a courthouse square, a marker everyone passes and nobody reads —
the stop becomes a note here, with the photographs we took and what we learned
standing there.
