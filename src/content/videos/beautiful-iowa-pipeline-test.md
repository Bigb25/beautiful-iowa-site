**This is a sample entry**, placed so the video shelf has its shape before the
first film is published.

Before any real episode, the pipeline had to prove itself end to end: one
photograph, one slow push-in, the channel title set in the masthead serif, and
the cream end card with the gold rule. Twenty seconds, rendered on our own
machine from a written composition — the same recipe every town film will
follow, with narration, captions, and the week's photographs in place of the
single frame.

When a film is published on the Beautiful Iowa channel, its page will live
here permanently: the video itself, the story behind it, and the sources for
everything it shows.
